# Sample Employee Handbook

This is fictional sample content for testing the local FAQ agent. It is not
official company policy.

## Time off

Full-time employees receive 15 days of paid time off per calendar year. Submit
planned time-off requests to your manager at least five business days in
advance. Emergency absences should be reported to your manager as soon as
practical.

## Learning budget

Employees may request up to $600 per calendar year for job-related books,
courses, or conferences. Submit a short description and estimated cost to your
manager for approval before purchasing.

## Working hours

The sample team collaborates between 10:00 a.m. and 3:00 p.m. local time.
Employees may arrange their remaining hours with their manager.
